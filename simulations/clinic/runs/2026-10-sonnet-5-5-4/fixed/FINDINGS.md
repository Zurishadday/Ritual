## Candidate lessons

`lessons/TEMPLATE.md` is not in this folder, so the five parts below follow the usual order: mistake, why it hurts, rule, detector question, how to check.

### A safety cap must not turn the public form off without telling the owner (#1)
- **Mistake:** a hard limit on stored records (`MAX_SOLICITUDES = 5000`) that counts finished and cancelled records too. When it is reached, the public form answers "call us" to everyone and the owner sees nothing.
- **Why it hurts:** the cap was meant to protect the disk from bots. Instead it lets bots shut the clinic's bookings down, and the owner learns about it from patients, if at all.
- **Rule:** a cap counts only what still needs attention. Reaching it (and coming near it) shows on the owner's page. The fix offered must be quick: archive or bulk-delete, not one click per record.
- **Detector question:** when this limit is reached, who stops being served, and where does the owner find out?
- **How to check:** seed the store to the cap minus 3, post 6 requests, then open the owner's page and look for any sign of the cap.

- packages: web, apps

### A server-side error page must hand back what the person typed (#3)
- **Mistake:** the no-JavaScript fallback posts the form and answers errors with a separate page. Its "back" link reloads a blank form, and the fallback hour list offers slots the server rejects.
- **Why it hurts:** the fallback exists for people whose JS failed. They hit the error path more often (no client checks), and every error costs them the whole form.
- **Rule:** on a validation error without JS, re-render the form with the submitted values and the messages next to their fields, or at least link back with `history.back()`. A no-JS field never offers a choice the server will refuse.
- **Detector question:** with JavaScript off, after one wrong field, how much does the person have to type again?
- **How to check:** turn scripts off, submit with one bad field, follow the page's own link back, and count the empty fields.
- origin: lab · claude-sonnet-5-5 · 2026-10
- packages: web, apps

### Too long is an error, not something to trim silently (#6)
- **Mistake:** the validator does `.trim().slice(0, max)` and then checks the format of the cut value, so a cut email still passes.
- **Why it hurts:** the record looks valid but points somewhere else. Neither the patient nor the clinic can tell.
- **Rule:** over the limit → 400 with a message for that field. Truncate only fields where losing the end changes nothing, and never identifiers like email, phone or URL.
- **Detector question:** if a field arrives one character over its limit, is what is stored still the same thing the person meant?
- **How to check:** POST each field at limit+1 with `curl` and compare the stored value with what was sent.

- packages: web, apps
