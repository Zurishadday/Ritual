# Code of conduct

Ritual is built from mistakes: ours, yours, and the models'. That only works if people can share them without fear.

- **Be kind about mistakes.** A shared mistake is a gift. Critique the lesson, never the person who made the mistake.
- **Be respectful.** No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, and no discrimination of any kind.
- **Protect privacy.** Never post someone else's private information, or a client's, a company's or a project's private data, even inside an example.
- **Assume good faith,** and when you disagree, say why.

The maintainer may edit or remove anything that breaks these rules, and block whoever keeps breaking them. To report a problem privately, use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
